## What is the Pearl Agent Playbook?
A comprehensive suite of resources dedicated to Agent Enablement. It will equip you with the strategies, training, and tools to bring home performance into your client conversations — and to put the insights from Pearl Home Performance Registry™ to work for your business.

## What is Pearl SCORE™?
Pearl SCORE™ is a 1-1,000 rating that reveals what listings and photos can't show—how well a home actually performs.

Pearl has built the first comprehensive home performance database in U.S. history: 97 million single-family homes now modeled, scored, and searchable on the Pearl Home Performance Registry™.

This isn't just a new tool — it's a new standard for the real estate industry.

For Your Buyers: Help them compare properties objectively, ask the right questions, and avoid costly surprises after closing.
For Your Sellers: Show them what today's buyers are looking for and how to showcase their home's performance to stand out in the market.

## Early Access Details
Early Access participants will receive hands-on support from Pearl staff to fully leverage Pearl's data insights about home performance and partner with Pearl to improve our tools and resources to meet your needs.

### What is an early access program?
The early access program is a collaborative phase where we work directly with a small group of trusted partners to ensure the Pearl Agent Playbook is effective, scalable, and truly useful in real-world scenarios.

### Who is accepted into the early access program?
We are focused on quality engagement over quantity of participants. To provide the best experience - and to ensure we are actively listening and have the capacity to act on those insights and put them into our product roadmap - we will be working with a small groups of agents and brokers in the beginning, and grow the number of participants over time.

### What is the goal of the early access program?
Pearl needs your real-world expertise to stress-test our materials, provide critical feedback, and ultimately help us build and perfect the Pearl Agent Playbook before its official launch.
